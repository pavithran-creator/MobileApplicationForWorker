TECHNICAL APPROACH
Full Stack • PostGIS • Gemini AI
📱 1. Client Access Layer
Next.js 14 Web & Expo Mobile
  • Next.js 14 App Router: Ultra-fast citizen booking portal with server route handlers.
  • Expo React Native: Field worker app with offline schedule caching and sound alerts.
  • 100% Bilingual: Instant toggling between English and Tamil (தமிழ் ta.json).
🧠 2. Intelligent Engine
Explainable Geo-Matching & NLP
  • 100-Point Formula: Skill (35) + Slot (20) + Distance (20) + Coop (10) + Cert (5) + Rating (10).
  • Interval Overlap Lock: max(s1, s2) < min(e1, e2) intercepts conflicts with HTTP 409.
  • AI Demand Forecasting: 60-day moving average with human admin review.
🗄️ 3. Civic Data Vault
Supabase PostgreSQL 15 + PostGIS
  • PostGIS Spatial Queries: True radial worker queries in under 50 milliseconds.
  • Row-Level Security (RLS): Cryptographic data isolation for citizen phone & addresses.
  • Tamper-Evident Audit: Immutable logging of all verifications and rebalances.
Multimodal Live Camera & Voice Booking + Explainable 100-Point Match AI MULTIMODAL
⚡ 4-Step Booking Lifecycle (Child-Friendly Flow)
  • Step 1 (Speak): Citizen speaks into mic: "Fan sparks at Gandhipuram 6 PM".
  • Step 2 (Evaluate): PostGIS evaluates closest available certified electricians (1.2 km away).
  • Step 3 (Slot Interlock): Overlap algorithm locks calendar; prevents double bookings.
  • Step 4 (Completion): Verified job done; 90% direct bank payout + audited invoice issued.
